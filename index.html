PaperPeak Support

PaperPeak scans paper into PDFs, recognizes the text on each page, and keeps everything on your iPhone.

Common questions

How do I scan? Tap + on the Documents tab and choose Scan with Camera. Hold the phone over the page; the edges are detected for you.

Can I import existing files? Yes. Tap + and choose Import from Photos or Import from Files. PDF files are added page by page.

How do I find text inside a document? Use the search field on the Documents tab. It searches titles and recognized text. To recognize text on older pages, open a document, tap Share, then Recognized Text.

How do I change the PDF page size? Settings → PDF Export → Page size.

Where are my documents stored? Only on your iPhone. Removing the app removes them, so export anything you want to keep.
