PaperPeak Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026.

PaperPeak is a document scanner for iPhone. It works on your device and does not collect any data about you.

What stays on your device

The pages you scan or import, the text recognized on them, and your settings are stored only in the app's storage on your iPhone. Text recognition runs on the device. Nothing is uploaded to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. PaperPeak has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party software that sends data off your device. The app does not make network requests.

Permissions

Camera: used only while you scan a document. Photos and files are read only when you pick them yourself in the system picker.

Sharing

A document leaves the app only when you export or share it, and it goes only to the destination you choose in the iOS share sheet.

Deleting your data

Delete a document in the app, use Settings → Delete All Documents, or remove the app. Each of these erases the data from your iPhone.

Children

PaperPeak does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue or see the Support page.
